My Learning Journey


The More You Build, the More You Learn


By Kenny Adeyemo · August 29, 2026




When I first started learning web development, I believed that watching tutorials was enough to help me become better. I would watch someone build a website and think I understood everything.


But when I started building projects on my own, I realized there was a big difference between watching someone write code and actually writing it yourself.


The more I build, the more I learn.


Learning Goes Beyond Tutorials


Tutorials are a great place to start. They introduce new concepts and show you how things work. But there are some lessons you only learn when you try to build something yourself.


When you start a project, you have to make decisions. You have to figure out how to structure your HTML, arrange elements with CSS, and fix things when they don't look the way you expected.


That is where the real learning begins.


You can watch someone build for hours, but building something yourself teaches you in a different way.


Every Project Teaches Something New


No matter how small a project is, there is always something new to figure out.


Sometimes, you start with a simple idea and realize there are parts of it you don't fully understand yet. That could be how to arrange a layout, make a design responsive, or get different elements to work together the way you imagined.


And that is one of the things I like about building. Each project pushes you to think, experiment, and learn something you may not have learned otherwise.


By the time you finish, you're not just left with a completed project — you also understand a little more than you did when you started.
